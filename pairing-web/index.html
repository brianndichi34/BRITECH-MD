HOW IT WORKS

Six quick steps. Then you’re in.

Keep this page open while you link your WhatsApp account.

  1. 01Add your number. Enter the WhatsApp number with its country code.
  2. 02Get your code. Tap “Link my number” and wait for the one-time code to appear.
  3. 03Open WhatsApp. Go to Settings → Linked Devices → Link a Device.
  4. 04Enter the code. Choose “Link with phone number instead”, then enter the code shown above.
  5. 05Check your DM. After linking, BRITECH-NB sends the SESSION_ID to the paired number.
  6. 06Explore your bot. Send .menu in WhatsApp to see the available commands.
